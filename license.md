**Wear is the interesting part**

Wear is what makes a cosmetic look used rather than generated. A factory-new item and
a battle-scarred one of the same skin read completely differently on screen. Being
able to set it per item is the difference between a plausible loadout and an obviously
staged one.

**What is client-side**

Everything. The changer repaints items on your own client. You see the result
immediately and in full detail. Other players see the item only if it exists on their
side, so treat the whole feature as a preview and styling tool.

**Building a loadout that looks real**

1. Pick a coherent theme rather than the most expensive item in each slot.
2. Set wear to match the theme — worn items suit a "used" loadout.
3. Keep one or two signature pieces, not seven.
4. Check it in a private lobby before a live match.

**What reverts**

Anything the server validates may reset after a completed match. Purely client-side
items persist until cleared. That behaviour is expected and no cosmetic overlay
changes it.
